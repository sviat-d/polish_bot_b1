Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Polish B1 exam?

The Polish B1 exam (Egzamin certyfikatowy z języka polskiego na poziomie B1) is an official certification exam that tests your Polish language skills at the B1 level of the Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR). It's often required for residency permits, citizenship, and academic purposes in Poland.

How does weak-topic training work?
